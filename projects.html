04 — Solo · VR Horror

Darker

Role: Solo Developer Engine: Unreal Engine 5 Platform: Meta Quest VR Year: 2025

A psychological horror experience built for VR. Players take on the role of Adrian Hale, exploring the abandoned Chamber Six of the Veyla Institute — only to discover they aren’t the original Adrian at all, but a manufactured consciousness trapped in an experimental loop. As the sole developer over a 13-week capstone, I built every system: a phase-managed state machine, VR interaction and raycast pickup, dynamic lighting with a battery-managed flashlight, a render-texture security-camera puzzle, and a dual-ending choice system. This was my first project in both Unreal Engine and VR.

06 — Playable · Group Project

Role: Programmer, Primary Artist, UI Designer Team: High Five (5 members) Engine: Godot 4 Released as BIRE

A 2D breeding simulation where players breed chickens backwards into microraptors to redeem a disgraced biologist’s career — inspired by Stardew Valley’s visuals and Wobbledogs’ genetics system. 01 — Capstone · Co-op Game

We Have A Problem

Role: Digital Designer Engine: Godot 4 Players: 2 Year: 2026

We Have A Problem is a two-player co-op sci-fi escape game about communicating clearly. One player sits at the ship’s console and sees the system reports, which are cryptic on their own. The other holds the manual and has to match each report to the right repair instruction, then talk their partner through the fix. Neither player can get through it alone.

It’s a game, but my part of the capstone was mostly digital design. I designed the look of the console interface, including the navigation, radar, engine, fuel, shields and retrieval-system screens, so each system reads clearly at a glance while still feeling like part of the same ship.

02 — Research · Worldbuilding

A World Designed: Soma

Role: Researcher & Writer Format: Presentation + process journal Year: 2026

Soma 1 is a cube-shaped ship, 5 km on each side, built in orbit as a revered ark mission to carry life to new worlds. It lost its course and has drifted for so long that the mission has become an abstraction. Food, water, power and waste all cycle through one closed loop, nobody has left since the route was lost, and the whole society runs on a single rule: maintain the ship.

This was a research and writing project to build a world and the logic that holds it together. Five floors house five castes named for the senses: the Seen, the Felt, the Heard, the Eaten and the Stench. A colour palette reads the ship from top to bottom, from institutional blue down to near-black, with red kept for Root, the outside. The research drew on Kowloon Walled City, the Universe 25 mouse experiment, caste systems and the “Wall” storyline from Solar Opposites. The presentation sets out the world, and the process journal shows how it developed.

01 — 3D Art · Rigging · Animation

Sky Kid

Role: Modeling, Rigging, Animation, Integration Tools: Blender, Unreal Engine Motion Design & Game Development Course

Inspired by Sky: Children of the Light, a fully modeled, rigged, and animated character taken from concept sketch to playable game asset. Bone structure and animation cycles (Bow, Light Wave, Run/Jump/Idle) were rendered from multiple angles, converted to 2D sprite sheets, and imported into Unreal as a 2.5D playable character — 3D fidelity with 2D performance.

02 — Animation · Blender

Motion Practice

Role: Animator Tools: Blender Course: ANFX211 Character Animation I Year: 2026

Three linked shots, each starring a different character who interacts with an object. The object arrives from the edge of the frame, is used or transformed by the character, then leaves the frame to enter the next shot. With the cameras locked off, everything has to come through the animation itself: timing, weight and the principles of animation. The shots were assembled into one sequence with sound.
