Evie Melhuish

Game Designer 3D Artist / Animator UI Designer

I’m a designer and developer with a BSc in Computer Science and Computer Graphics and Games, now completing a Graduate Diploma in Design Innovation at Victoria University of Wellington. I work across the creative pipeline — game design, 3D modelling and animation, UI and digital design — and pick up new tools quickly. Most recently I led three developers and three artists to ship The King’s Messenger. I release my games under the name BIRE — short for Bionic Reader.
