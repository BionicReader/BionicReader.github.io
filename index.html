I’m a designer and developer with a BSc in Computer Science and Computer Graphics and Games, now completing a Graduate Diploma in Design Innovation at Victoria University of Wellington. I work across the creative pipeline, from game design to 3D modelling and animation to UI and digital design, and I pick up new tools quickly. Most recently I led three developers and three artists to ship The King’s Messenger. I release my games under the name BIRE, short for Bionic Reader.
