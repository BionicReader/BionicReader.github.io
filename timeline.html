    A Godot breeding-sim group project, and a full character pipeline — modeled, rigged, and animated in Blender, then integrated as a playable 2.5D character in Unreal.

  8. 2023

    Started BSc, Victoria University of Wellington

    Enrolled in an introductory game design paper on a whim — it became my highest grade and set the direction for everything since.

  9. 2022

    Carlien Calvert Prize & Raukaraka Scholarship

    First in Visual Art (Photography), and a Victoria University scholarship for leadership and community work.
